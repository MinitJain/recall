# Ritro web surfaces

Scope: landing (Persuade), auth, dashboard, settings, legal, 404 and loading states (Operate/Read). Direction pinned by the user: light, editorial, after aatmanjain.com. Warm off-white, elegant serif, one restrained accent, clean spacing, consistent tokens. Dark mode kept. Tagline kept: "Save anything. Find everything."

Audience and job: heavy savers who lose what they save. Action: create an account (landing), save and find (app). Proof: real product UI and real mechanism only; no testimonials or counts exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ritro is a personal archive that returns things to you. The surface is a reading room's lending desk, not a SaaS hero: the category default (gradient hero, three feature cards, glowing mock) is refused.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground #F4EFE6 with ink #1F1B16; stamp-pad violet #3E3A9C as the single accent, used for date stamps, links and the primary action. Source Serif 4 for headings and lead copy, Geist for UI, Geist Mono only for dates and counts. Hairline rules, no cards on the landing, square-ish 6px corners in the app.

STORY: the visitor sees one saved link come back with its date stamp, understands that saving is instant and the archive resurfaces things, and signs up from the hero.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left two thirds: serif headline "Save anything. Find everything.", one line of lead, a primary "Start your archive" button and a secondary "Add the extension" link. Right third: a returned item slip, a real-looking saved link with tags and two violet date stamps, SAVED and BACK.

FORM: library return slip with date stamps as the signature move, carried into the app as the saved-date stamp on each bookmark. Position 1 of the user's pinned brief. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
